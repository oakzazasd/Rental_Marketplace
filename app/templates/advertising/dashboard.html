How pricing works

  • Advertising costs are calculated per day from a base price plus two multipliers.
  • Category competition: the more active listings a category has, the higher the competition multiplier (1.0× → 1.25× → 1.5× → 2.0×).
  • Promotion level: Basic 1.0× (+10 ranking), Standard 1.5× (+25 ranking), Premium 2.0× (+50 ranking).
  • Promoted items always show a Sponsored label and can never outrank a strongly relevant search result.
  • This version simulates the purchase — no real payment provider is connected yet.
